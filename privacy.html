Privacy Policy – Marvis

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Marvis is a personal content assistant used only by the owner of the “Syahrul Codes” channels to prepare short coding tutorial videos and publish them to the owner’s own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram). Marvis is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account, Marvis may access basic account information (such as display name and open ID) and the permission to upload or publish videos to that account. Marvis does not access followers, private messages, or other users’ data.

How information is used

The information is used only to upload videos created by Marvis to the connected account, after the owner approves them.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner’s private computer and are never shared with third parties. No data is sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

The owner can disconnect Marvis at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Locally stored tokens are deleted when the connection is removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: syahrulhajji@gmail.com
